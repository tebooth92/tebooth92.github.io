Calculator Logic & FAQ

Calculation Logic

Pay is calculated by combining the Base Pay Rate (or Casual Rate) with any selected allowances. All components are summed to determine the total hourly rate.

Higher Duties / Over Awards

Percentage increases are applied to the Base Pay Rate only. They typically do not include other allowances unless specifically configured.

Superannuation

Calculated at 12% of ordinary time earnings, capped at the employer obligation maximum of $29,932.20 per year. This is the maximum amount the employer is required to contribute on the employee's behalf.

Date Ranges

Each classification can have multiple effective date ranges as EBAs roll over. The most recent range is selected by default; pick an older range to calculate against historical rates. Mental Health rates from Salary Circular 880 are converted to hourly using 38 hours/week (casual = base + 25%); verify against the underlying EBA. Only Laundry, Uniform, and Qualification allowances from the MH circular are surfaced.

+1 Week Age Loading Applied
