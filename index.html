Calculator Logic & FAQ

Calculation Logic

Pay is calculated by combining the Base Pay Rate or Casual Rate (one or the other) with any selected allowances. Percentage allowances are a percentage of the base rate. All components are summed to determine the total hourly rate.

Higher Duties / Over Awards

An existing over award is entered as an amount and frequency and converted to hourly using the configured Hours per Week. An increase can be calculated by matching a target classification, by a percentage (of base pay + existing over award by default, or base only, or all selected components), by a fixed amount, or by targeting a total package. The summary shows the old and new over award and the variance.

Superannuation

Calculated at 12% of the full total (all selected components, existing over award and any increase), capped at the maximum contribution base for the current financial year ($30,000.00 for 2025-26, $32,499.60 from 1 July 2026).

Progression Badges

Badges under the selected classification come from the payroll system: whether the pay grade auto-progresses, is at the top of its range, and the next pay scale level (click it to load that classification). A casual classification badge marks bank and casual-only codes, which carry a Casual Rate instead of a Base Pay Rate.

Date Ranges

Each classification can have multiple effective date ranges as EBAs roll over. The most recent range is selected by default; pick an older range to calculate against historical rates. Mental Health rates from Salary Circular 880 are converted to hourly using 38 hours/week. Current rates were reconciled against the payroll system export in September 2026; future-dated ranges remain as published in the salary circulars. Qualification allowances and experience payments are published weekly and are shown as hourly components (weekly divided by 38).
