Current schedules (.xlsx)

Files already loaded in the Pre Payment Audit are picked up automatically. Cardholder dates (Created, Modified, Termination) are read as US format (M/D/YYYY). Terminated or deleted cardholders are excluded.

Employees

How this is worked out: a cardholder counts as onboarded when Status is Complete, they are not terminated or deleted, and their ClientAccountKey appears in an Active ME or LE report. Each card's EAID is then looked for as a Bank Ref on an EMLMEC (ME card) or EMLEBC (LE card) line with a Fortnightly Amount above zero. Employees who have maximised will also show no schedule; use the date window, Created date and card balance to tell them apart.

Terminated cards still scheduled

EMLMEC / EMLEBC lines whose Bank Ref is not an active card. Not limited by the date window.
