Fee Audit

Checks every employee with Living Expenses or Meal Entertainment benefits collects the full FBT-year fee. Employees absent from the User Management Employee List are treated as terminated and excluded.

Remaining pay cycles are derived per line from its Next Payment Date - fortnights counted from the next payment through 31 March. This self-corrects around pay runs and handles the H/M calendar offset.

Benefits Card Audit

Reconciles the EML card program: EMLMEC lines against the Active ME report, EMLEBC lines against the Active LE report (EAID + employee ID must both match), proxy card integrity, card usage, and card fee lines.

Held Balance Report

Employees with a held balance (Employer Package List, column G), banded into three tiers, plus a Return to Payroll tab: employees whose held balance exceeds their remaining cap entitlement (Capped Amount Available + EB Capped Amount Available; blank = full entitlement of $9,009.96 / $2,649.98), meaning they have been over-deducted. Export produces one workbook with all four tabs.

Novated Post-Tax Split

Reads the Calculated Shortfall from the post-tax audit report (negative = under-collected) and proposes the revised post-tax / pre-tax split over the remaining pay cycles. The post-tax increase is always rounded UP to the next cent so collection never falls short, and the pre-tax line is decreased by the same amount so the two deposits still sum to the NL Finance payment.

Novated Lease Variance

Vehicle Next Deposit less Lease Rental Payment should always equal $4.89 (the fortnightly NL admin fee). Flags any other variance, leases past their end date still on file ("Cease Novated Lease"), and clears intentional once-off catch-ups (a deposit+payment NL Finance line in benefit details).

Opportunity

Active employees (Employee Central) on Fortnightly General or Fortnightly Medical who are not salary packaging: no payment ever in the finance batch and nothing set up in benefit details. Also flags employees registered in the salary packaging system with no deductions set up and no LE/ME payments.
