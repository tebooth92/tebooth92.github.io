Edit Card

Each card represents one process step in your workflow.

i
🏷️
A short, active-verb phrase naming exactly what happens in this step. Keep it concise — aim for 2–5 words. It appears as the card header.
"Approve Purchase Order" · "Notify Customer" · "Validate Form"
i
👤
The person, team, or role responsible for carrying out this step. Use a job title or team name — not a specific person's name. Shown as a badge on the card.
"HR Advisor" · "Line Manager" · "Finance Team" · "Customer Service"
i
📝
A plain-English explanation of what is done in this step, who does it, and any key rules or decisions made. This is the body text of the card.
"Manager reviews the submitted timesheet and confirms hours are correct before passing to payroll."
i
📥
The information, documents, or items this step needs to begin. These are the triggers or materials that feed into this process step. One item per line — they appear on the left side of the card.
"Signed contract" · "Customer ID" · "Completed order form"
i
📤
The information, decisions, or items this step produces when done. These become the inputs for downstream steps. One item per line — they appear on the right side of the card.
"Approved invoice" · "Confirmation email" · "Updated database record"
